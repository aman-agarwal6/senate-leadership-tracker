How the tracker counts votes

Public positions reflect statements or attributed reporting. The candidates count in their own columns. A call for new leadership belongs under “Wants change / no pick” until the senator names a candidate.

Research leans are tentative judgments based on the evidence in a senator’s profile. Policy alignment alone is too weak to assign a lean. The ballot is private, and public positions can change.

Scenarios start with public candidate support and incorporate your picks. A pick for a conditional seat adds that seat to the scenario. Inferred leans are never added automatically.

Source links are listed in each profile alongside the evidence they support.
